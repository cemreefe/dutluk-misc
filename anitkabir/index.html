ANITKABİR

Inscription capitals digitized from the lettering Emin Barın cut into the walls of Atatürk's mausoleum in 1952 (Gençliğe Hitabe and the 10th-Year Speech tablets).

Traced from Metin Akın's 2015 FontLab Studio digitization poster (Hacettepe University, GRA666).

Coverage: A–Z except Q W X, plus Ç Ğ İ Ö Ş Ü and period. Lowercase maps to the same capitals. No digits or other punctuation.
